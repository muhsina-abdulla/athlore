# Product Vision

Athlore is intended to become a long-term training intelligence system for my MMA journey.

It should bring together my training journal, progress, goals, milestones, analytics, and accumulated knowledge so that I can understand my development over time.

## Understand my training

Athlore should help me see my training as a whole rather than as a collection of individual sessions.

I should be able to understand:

- What I have been training
- How consistently I have been training
- Which techniques and skills I have worked on
- What I have struggled with
- What I have improved
- What patterns exist across my training history

## Make progress visible

Progress in MMA is often gradual and difficult to see from day to day.

Athlore should make that progress visible through training history, meaningful statistics, goals, and milestones.

Each goal should be able to have its own progression path, allowing me to see where I am, what I have completed, and what I am working toward next.

The milestone progression should make long-term development visual and motivating.

## Turn training history into knowledge

Athlore should allow me to actively learn from my accumulated training history.

I should be able to retrieve things I have previously learned, revisit relevant coach feedback, identify recurring areas for improvement, and ask questions about my own training.

The system should distinguish between:

- **Recorded information** — what actually exists in my training history
- **Analysis** — patterns or observations derived from that history
- **Suggestions** — ideas generated to help me think about my training

This distinction is important because Athlore should never present an assumption as something that actually happened in training.

## Help me look forward

Athlore should eventually help connect past training with future training.

My training history should be useful when deciding what to revisit, what areas may need more attention, and how I might approach an upcoming session or goal.

Athlore should support my decision-making, not replace my coaches or make training decisions for me.

## Long-term direction

Athlore is being built around my own MMA training first.

As the product evolves, I will consider which parts of the system should become more configurable and whether the same ideas could be useful for other athletes or training styles.

The long-term goal is to have one place where I can keep track of my training, understand my progress, and use what I have learned to improve over time.
